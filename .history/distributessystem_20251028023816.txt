1 a distributed system is a collection of independent computers that appear to users as a single coherent system or 
in other words a distributed system is a system in which components hardware or software located at networked computers 
communicate and coordinate their actions by passing messages
2 distributed systems are used to share resources, improve performance, enhance reliability, and provide scalability.
3 distributed computing is a field of computer science that studies distributed systems and the algorithms and protocols
that enable them to function effectively.
4 distributed system architecture is the design and organization of a distributed system, including the hardware and software components,
the communication protocols, and the algorithms used to coordinate the actions of the components.
5 distributed system model is a conceptual framework that describes the structure and behavior of a distributed system.
6 distributed system components are the individual hardware or software components that make up a distributed system.
7 distributed system services are the functions and capabilities that a distributed system provides to its users.
8 distributed system protocols are the rules and conventions that govern the communication between the components of a distributed system 
widely used in grpc .

1 why distributed systems are hard to design and implement?
ans distributed systems are hard to design and implement because they involve many complex issues, such as communication delays, network failures, 
data concurrency(excution of multiple computaion on same piece of data might interfere with each other ) and partial component failures.
distributed systems are also hard to design and implement because they require a deep understanding of computer science 
concepts such as algorithms, data structures, and network protocols.

2 what is partitioning in distributed systems?
ans partitioning in distributed systems is the process of dividing a large dataset into small
er, more manageable pieces and distributing them across multiple nodes in a network.
partitioning is used to improve the performance and scalability of distributed systems by allowing multiple nodes to process data in parallel.
